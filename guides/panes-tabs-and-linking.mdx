# Panes, Tabs, and Linking

The workspace is made from panes. A pane can contain one tab or a group of tabs.

## Pane types

The beta includes:

- **Chart**
- **Orderbook**
- **Time & Sales**
- **Indicator**
- **Trade**
- **Backtester**

Use the quick-add icons in the lower left rail to spawn them.

## Working with tabs

Pane tabs behave like browser tabs:

- Click a tab to activate it
- Drag tabs to reorder them
- Drop a tab onto another tab or the center of a pane to group them
- Drop near an edge to split the workspace in that direction
- Drag a grouped tab away to detach it into its own pane
- Press `Escape` to cancel an active drag

Grouped tabs remain mounted while inactive. Their chart state and loaded data do not need to start from zero every time you switch back.

## Selected pane

Click anywhere inside a pane to select it. The selected-pane toolbar directly below the top bar now targets that pane.

This matters before changing:

- Market
- Timeframe
- Chart style
- Heatmap
- Indicators
- Chart settings
- Link state

## Linked panes

A linked pane follows shared market changes. This is useful for a chart, Orderbook, and tape that should always show the same symbol.

Changing a market or timeframe from a pane-specific control can unlink that pane so it can operate independently. Use the link control in the selected-pane toolbar to restore or remove linking.

## Sending markets from the screener

The Markets screener supports two routes:

- Click a row to send the market to the selected pane
- Click **ALL** to send it to every compatible pane

Use **ALL** carefully if some panes are intentionally unlinked.
