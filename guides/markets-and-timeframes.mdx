# Markets and Timeframes

Market and timeframe controls appear in the selected-pane toolbar.

## Aggregated markets

The **AGG** tab contains:

- BTC
- ETH
- SOL

Aggregated markets combine supported venue data and are the main TradeNet order-flow products.

## Per-exchange markets

The native desktop terminal also exposes linear perpetual markets from:

- Binance
- Bybit
- OKX
- Hyperliquid

Choose the venue tab, then select a market. Per-exchange panes are useful when you need to isolate one venue instead of reading the combined market.

## Preset timeframes

The standard list includes:

- 15 seconds
- 30 seconds
- 1 minute
- 5 minutes
- 15 minutes
- 1 hour
- 4 hours

## Custom timeframes

Open the timeframe menu, enter a number, select seconds, minutes, hours, or days, then add it.

Valid custom timeframes run from 1 second to 30 days.

There is one important rule:

- Sub-minute timeframes are live-only
- Timeframes of 1 minute or higher must be whole-minute multiples

For example, 7 minutes and 2 hours are valid. A 90-second interval is rejected because exact historical candles cannot be reconstructed by splitting a 1-minute source candle.

## History behavior

Minute-based custom timeframes are built from a canonical interval that divides evenly into the requested display interval. Flow metrics are summed. Level and cumulative metrics use the final source value in the combined bar.

Heatmaps automatically request a visual resolution appropriate to the chart scale. A 4-hour chart does not need to paint every 1-minute heatmap frame.
