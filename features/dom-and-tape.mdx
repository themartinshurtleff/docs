# Orderbook and Time & Sales

The Orderbook pane shows resting depth. Time & Sales shows completed trades.

Use them together to compare intent with execution.

## Add the panes

Use the quick-add buttons in the left rail:

- **Add Depth pane** opens the user-facing Orderbook
- **Add Tape pane** opens Time & Sales

Keep both linked to a chart if you want all three to follow the same market.

## Orderbook pane

The Orderbook is a price ladder with bids, asks, spread information, contribution bars, and cumulative depth.

Quick controls include:

- Recenter and autofollow
- Base, USD, or combined size display
- Minimum USD fade
- Tick grouping
- Depth-window level count
- Visible-row density

Settings include:

- Aggregated or per-venue split mode
- Combined or individual exchange heat
- Venue selection
- Spread marker colors
- Row height
- Frame rate
- Delta update cadence
- Compact or dimmed labels
- Order-block bar colors, opacity, and scale
- Cumulative depth band, edge, colors, and opacity
- Turbo hover behavior

Aggregated books can appear crossed because grouped prices from separate venues overlap. TradeNet labels this honestly instead of forcing the view to resemble one venue's book.

## Time & Sales

The tape shows:

- Time
- Price
- Size
- Buy or sell side
- Source exchange

Minimum-notional filters include:

- All trades
- $10,000
- $50,000
- $100,000
- $250,000
- $1,000,000

Large trades receive stronger visual emphasis.

## Reading them together

Ask:

- Is aggressive flow hitting a visible wall?
- Is the wall holding, replenishing, or disappearing?
- Are large prints moving price?
- Is the same behavior concentrated on one venue?
- Does the footprint confirm what the tape showed?

<Note>
The public beta documentation does not describe click-to-trade behavior from the ladder because that is not the normal supported execution path. Place and manage orders from the Trade pane and bottom dock.
</Note>
