# Markets Screener

Open **Markets** from the left rail.

## Venue tabs

The screener has:

- **AGG**
- **BIN**
- **BYB**
- **OKX**
- **HL**

AGG shows the supported aggregated BTC, ETH, and SOL products. The other tabs show linear perpetual markets from the selected venue.

## Market rows

Rows show:

- Symbol
- Current price
- Percentage change
- 24-hour volume

Per-exchange lists prioritize higher-volume contracts, with favorites kept near the top.

## Favorites

Use the star on a row to add or remove a favorite.

Favorites are also respected by the selected-pane market menu.

## Send to a pane

Click a market row to send it to the currently selected pane.

Use **ALL** to send that market to all compatible panes.

This makes it quick to move an entire linked order-flow workspace from BTC to ETH or from an aggregate to one venue.
