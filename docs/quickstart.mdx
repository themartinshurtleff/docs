# Quick Start

This guide walks you through a realistic first session. Login to a working liquidation heatmap in a few minutes.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in">
    Launch the terminal and sign in with your TradeNet account email and password. This is the account from the website waitlist or beta signup. The terminal requires login. No account yet? See [Installation](/docs/installation).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a pair">
    Open the symbol picker and select **AGGREGATED BTC**.

    Aggregated pairs combine order flow from every connected exchange into a single stream. Per-exchange pairs show one venue only. Aggregated BTC, ETH, and SOL are the flagship pairs and the best place to start. The thousands of per-exchange pairs across Binance, Bybit, OKX, and Hyperliquid are there when you need a specific venue or a smaller cap.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a layout">
    Pick a layout template: **1 Chart**, **2 Columns**, **2 Rows**, **Focus + 2**, **4 Grid**, or **Flow Desk**. Start with 1 Chart or Focus + 2. You can switch any time, and your layout persists between sessions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add panes">
    Each pane can be a chart (candlestick, footprint, or heatmap), a DOM ladder, a Time & Sales tape, or a screener. A solid first setup: candlestick chart in the main pane, DOM and tape beside it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on the liquidation heatmap">
    Enable the liquidation heatmap overlay on your chart. Bright zones are dense liquidation clusters. Long clusters sit below price, short clusters above. Price gravitates toward them.

    Read [Reading the Liquidation Heatmap](/docs/guides/reading-the-heatmap) to learn how to trade them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Optional: connect Bitunix to trade">
    Market data needs no API keys. Trading does. Connect a Bitunix account to execute directly from the terminal with market, limit, and advanced algo orders. See [Bitunix Connect](/docs/integrations/bitunix-connect).
  </Step>
</Steps>
