# Footprint Charts

Real-time footprint charts aggregated across multiple exchanges. Trades are combined server-side, so a footprint loads in well under a second instead of your machine crunching raw trades locally.

## What you get

Footprint charts break each candle into its individual price levels and show you the buy volume, sell volume, and trade counts at every level. Standard candlestick charts tell you open, high, low, close. Footprint charts tell you what actually happened inside each bar.

On aggregated pairs, trades from Binance, Bybit, OKX, and Hyperliquid are merged server-side before they reach your screen. You are not looking at one exchange. You are looking at the combined order flow from four.

Footprints also work on per-exchange pairs if you want single-venue data. Aggregated pairs are the recommended default.

## Tick grouping

Price levels are grouped into configurable tick sizes. Defaults on aggregated pairs:

| Symbol | Default grouping |
|---|---|
| BTC | $5 |
| ETH | $0.50 |
| SOL | $0.05 |

Widen the grouping to read structure at a glance. Tighten it to inspect individual levels.

## Candlestick charts

Full candlestick charting with custom timeframes. Pick standard intervals or set your own. The chart engine is built for speed. No lag, no waiting for candles to populate.

## Aggregated CVD

Cumulative Volume Delta tracks the running difference between aggressive buying and aggressive selling. TradeNet aggregates CVD across exchanges so you see the true net aggression, not just what is happening on Binance or Bybit alone.

When CVD diverges from price, pay attention. That divergence often signals that the move is running out of fuel.

## Bar stats panel

Every bar on the chart has a stats panel with 9 rows of data:

| Metric | What it tells you |
|---|---|
| Volume | Total volume traded in the bar |
| Buy Vol | Aggressive buy volume (market buys) |
| Sell Vol | Aggressive sell volume (market sells) |
| Delta | Net difference between buy vol and sell vol |
| 24h CVD | 24-hour cumulative volume delta |
| OI Delta | Change in open interest during the bar |
| OI CVD | Cumulative open interest delta |
| Short Liq | Short liquidation volume in the bar |
| Long Liq | Long liquidation volume in the bar |

Nine rows of context on every single bar. No switching tabs. No opening separate tools.

<Note>
On aggregated pairs, bar stats are computed server-side. Every user sees identical numbers, regardless of when they loaded the chart or what their connection looks like.
</Note>

## Volume Profile

Volume profile tools are live. Fixed Range and Anchored Volume Profile build volume-at-price histograms from the same footprint data, with POC, value area, and buy/sell splits. See [Drawing Tools & Volume Profile](/docs/features/drawing-tools) for the full breakdown.
