# DOM Ladder & Time and Sales

The DOM shows you what is waiting. The tape shows you what is happening. Together they are the closest you get to the raw market.

## The DOM ladder

A vertical price ladder with resting bid and ask depth at every level, plus recent traded volume by level so you can see where size has already changed hands.

- **Tick grouping.** Group levels into configurable tick sizes. Tighten for precision, widen for structure.
- **Recenter.** Snap the ladder back to current price with one action when the market runs away from your view.
- **Size grading.** Large resting orders are visually graded so walls stand out without you scanning every number.

The ladder is available per exchange (Binance, Bybit, OKX, Hyperliquid) and for aggregated pairs, where depth from all venues combines into one ladder.

## Trading from the DOM

The ladder is also an execution surface. If your account is connected, you trade directly from it:

| Action | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Click a price | Places a limit order there |
| Shift + click | Places a stop order |
| Drag an order | Reprices it |

Setup and gating are covered in [Trading from the Terminal](/docs/trading/getting-started).

## Time & Sales

The tape prints every trade as it happens: time, price, size, side, and the source exchange. No sampling, no summarizing. Every print.

- **Size filtering.** Hide trades below a size threshold. Retail noise disappears, real flow stays.
- **Pause and freeze.** Stop the scroll to inspect a burst of prints without losing them.
- **Large-trade highlighting.** Big prints get flagged visually so you catch them even when the tape is moving fast.

<Note>
On aggregated pairs, the tape merges all venues into one stream. Each print keeps its exchange label, so you always know which venue a trade came from.
</Note>

## How to use them together

Watch the ladder for where size is resting. Watch the tape for whether aggressive flow is hitting it. A wall on the DOM that absorbs print after print on the tape is holding. A wall that vanishes the moment the tape speeds up was never real. That read takes seconds once both are in front of you.
