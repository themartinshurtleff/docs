# Introduction

TradeNet Terminal is a native desktop trading terminal for crypto derivatives. Built in Rust with the Iced GUI framework. GPU-rendered via wgpu. Sub-1ms exchange latency. It aggregates order flow from Binance, Bybit, OKX, and Hyperliquid into a single interface, and executes trades natively on Bitunix.

Currently in closed beta (v0.8.6). Available on Windows and macOS.

## The Problem

Other platforms use static liquidation models. They estimate where liquidations sit, but those models do not adapt. When market conditions shift, the model stays the same. You are trading on stale projections.

That is not good enough.

## The Solution

TradeNet Terminal runs a self-calibrating liquidation prediction algorithm. It models open interest distribution and projects liquidation clusters before price reaches them. Real forced liquidation events from Binance, Bybit, and OKX feed back into the model continuously, adjusting its leverage weight distribution in real time. Static models decay. This one stays accurate.

You see the targets. You position ahead of the move.

## What's in the Terminal

The liquidation heatmap is the flagship. It is not the whole product.

**Charting.** Candlestick, footprint, comparison, and heatmap charts. Multiple layouts, popout windows for multi-monitor setups, theme support.

**Order flow.** Orderbook heatmap, DOM ladder, Time & Sales, open interest tools, and bar stats with 9 server-computed rows per bar.

**Drawing tools.** Full drawing suite including Fixed Range and Anchored Volume Profile, plus TPO.

**Custom indicators.** Write your own in Lua. Sandboxed scripting with full access to the terminal's data.

**Trading.** Native execution on Bitunix. Market and limit orders plus advanced algo orders: Scaled, TWAP, Chase, Iceberg, and Swarm. The Order Management Center tracks everything. Panic close flattens you in one click.

## Symbols and Exchanges

Aggregated BTC, ETH, and SOL are the flagship pairs. They combine order flow from every connected exchange into a single stream. Beyond those, thousands of per-exchange pairs are available for charting across Binance, Bybit, OKX, and Hyperliquid.

- **Binance** - USDT and COIN-M perpetuals
- **Bybit** - Linear and inverse perpetuals
- **OKX** - USDT-M and COIN-M swaps
- **Hyperliquid** - All listed perpetuals (trades/orderflow only)

The liquidation heatmap backend collects data from Binance, Bybit, and OKX via WebSocket. Hyperliquid is connected on the frontend for trades and orderflow. All data streams simultaneously. No API keys needed for market data. The terminal handles connection management and reconnection automatically.

## Tech Stack

The terminal is built from scratch. No Electron. No web wrapper.

- **Rust + Iced GUI framework** for the native desktop frontend
- **wgpu** as the rendering backend for Iced's canvas (GPU-accelerated charts)
- **Python/FastAPI backend on Hetzner** for server-side data processing and aggregation
- **Custom protocol** for compressed market data streaming

Everything is optimized for speed. The rendering pipeline runs on your GPU. Data processing runs on dedicated servers. Your machine handles display. The servers handle the math.
