TradeNet Terminal has native execution built in. You analyze, place, and manage orders in the same window. Your charts, your DOM, your orders, one screen.

<Warning>
Trading leveraged futures involves substantial risk. You can lose more than your initial margin. Nothing in these docs is financial advice. Size accordingly.
</Warning>

## Requirements

Before you can place orders, you need:

<Steps>
  <Step title="A Bitunix Futures account connected via API keys">
    Bitunix is the only execution venue for now. More exchanges are planned. Follow the [Bitunix connection guide](/docs/integrations/bitunix-connect) to generate keys and link your account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Affiliate verification or a paid plan">
    Order placement is gated. Either verify your Bitunix account under the TradeNet affiliate link, or subscribe to a paid plan. Charting works without either.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
This gating applies to every order type. Market, limit, advanced algos, TP/SL, all of it goes through the same check.
</Note>

Both hedge mode and one-way mode are supported. Leverage is set per symbol from the trade panel.

## Ways to trade

### The trade panel

The trade panel lives in the sidebar. It contains:

- **Order form.** Side, size, price. Pick market or limit, attach TP/SL, set flags.
- **Leverage and margin controls.** Set leverage per symbol. Switch margin mode.
- **Account summary.** Balance, used margin, open PnL at a glance.

### Chart trading

Your position lives on the chart. Entry, take profit, stop loss, and liquidation lines are drawn directly on it.

- Drag a TP or SL line to move it. The order updates on the exchange.
- Click the chart to autofill that price into the order form. Faster than typing.

### DOM ladder trading

The DOM (depth of market) ladder gives you one-click execution at exact prices:

| Action | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Left-click a price | Places a limit order there |
| Shift + click | Places a stop order |
| Ctrl + click | Places a position-linked stop |
| Drag an order | Reprices it |

### The bottom dock

The Order Management Center is a dock along the bottom of the terminal. It holds your positions, open orders, history, and running strategies. See [Order Management Center](/docs/trading/order-management).

## Risk-based sizing

Stop guessing contract counts. Enter your risk as a percentage of account or a flat currency amount, and the terminal computes position size for you:

```
size = risk / |entry - stop|
```

Set your stop where it belongs on the chart, type your risk, and the size field fills itself. Same risk per trade, every trade.

<Tip>
Risk-based sizing pairs well with chart trading. Drag your stop line, and the computed size updates with it.
</Tip>

## Paper mode

Want to practice first? Enable paper mode. The terminal simulates fills without sending anything to the exchange. Use it to learn the panels, test [advanced orders](/docs/trading/advanced-orders), or rehearse a strategy before risking capital.
