The Order Management Center is a full-width dock at the bottom of the terminal. It is resizable, collapsible, and independent of your chart layout. Change your panes all you want, the dock stays put.

<Note>
The venue is the source of truth. Every number in the dock comes from the exchange, not from local estimates. What you see is what Bitunix says you have.
</Note>

## Feed health

A status dot shows the data connection:

| Color | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Green | Live websocket feed |
| Yellow | Degraded. Fell back to REST polling. Algos continue running |
| Red | Disconnected |

## Symbol filter

Show all contracts, or only the symbol of the current pane. Useful when you trade several markets and want the dock to follow your focus.

</Tip>

### Order History

Past orders with status (Filled, Canceled, Expired, Partially Filled and Canceled), average fill price, filled quantity shown as "k of N", and order ID. Paginated with load more.

### Trade History

Individual fills: price, quantity, maker or taker role, fee paid, realized PnL, trade ID. This is where you audit your actual execution quality.

### Position History

Closed positions: average entry, average exit, closing PnL, open and close times.

The Details drawer shows the full parameter recap (the locked snapshot you confirmed at placement), a child order ledger, and an event log of everything the algo did.

## Panic

The emergency exit. Panic closes **all pending orders** (manual, algo children, TP/SL) and **all open positions**, for one symbol or for everything.

<Warning>
Panic is total. It cancels every order and market-closes every position in its scope, including stops protecting other trades. Positions are closed at market, so expect slippage in fast conditions. There is no undo.
</Warning>

To prevent fat-finger disasters, Panic uses a two-click confirm on the same button. Click once, the button changes to "Sure?". Click again within about 3 seconds to execute. The dock shows progress while orders cancel and positions close.

Use it when something is wrong and you want flat, now. For routine exits, use Flash Close or a reduce-only order instead.
