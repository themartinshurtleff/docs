# Quick Start

This guide gets you from a fresh install to a usable order-flow workspace. It does not try to teach the entire terminal at once.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Launch TradeNet Terminal and sign in with your TradeNet account. Leave **Remember me** enabled on a trusted personal machine.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start with aggregated BTC">
    Select the chart pane, open the market menu in the selected-pane toolbar, choose **AGG**, then choose **BTC**.

    Aggregated BTC combines supported venues and has the broadest set of TradeNet data layers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose split or canvas mode">
    Use the layout buttons near the bottom of the left rail.

    **Docked split** keeps panes in a resizable grid. **Freeform canvas** lets you place and resize panes on a larger scrollable surface.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add an orderbook and tape">
    Use the quick-add buttons on the left rail to add an **Orderbook** pane and a **Time & Sales** pane.

    Keep the panes linked if you want them to follow the selected market.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure the chart">
    Set the chart style to **Candlesticks** or **Footprint**. Add **Footprint Bar Stats**, **Daily CVD**, and **Open Interest** from the indicator browser.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on a heatmap">
    Choose **Liquidation** or **Orderbook** from the chart heatmap control. Open the settings gear to adjust theme, resolution, intensity, opacity, and minimum notional.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the workspace">
    Open **WORKSPACES** in the top bar, name the current setup, and save it. Star the template if you want it to load when TradeNet starts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Try a paper order">
    Open the Trade panel from the left rail. Confirm the panel shows simulated or paper mode, choose a small margin amount, review the order preview, and place a market or limit order.
  </Step>
</Steps>
