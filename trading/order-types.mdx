# Order Types and Risk Controls

The manual Trade ticket supports market and limit orders with shared sizing and risk controls.

## Market orders

A market order enters or reduces a simulated position at the current paper execution price.

Use it when immediate execution matters more than controlling the exact entry.

## Limit orders

A limit order rests at your selected price until the simulated market reaches it or you cancel it.

Use it when the entry price matters more than immediate execution.

## Buy and sell

- **Buy** opens or increases a long position
- **Sell** opens or increases a short position

If an opposing position already exists, the result depends on size and reduce-only state.

## Reduce-only

Reduce-only prevents the order from increasing exposure. Use it for exits and partial closes.

## Leverage

The leverage control changes paper notional relative to the entered margin USD.

Higher leverage increases exposure and brings the estimated liquidation price closer. It does not make a setup better.

## Margin mode

- **Isolated** assigns margin to the selected position
- **Cross** models shared account margin

The beta simulation exposes both workflows. Exact exchange liquidation and account rules can differ from the simulator.

## Take profit

The manual ticket supports up to five take-profit levels with allocation percentages.

Make sure allocations describe how much of the position should close at each target.

## Stop loss

The ticket supports one stop-loss level for the position.

Review the stop direction before confirming:

- A long stop belongs below the relevant market price
- A short stop belongs above it

## Confirmation

TradeNet shows a confirmation step before submitting the order. Read it.

Check:

- Market
- Side
- Order type
- Margin USD
- Leverage
- Notional
- Quantity
- Limit price
- Reduce-only
- TP and SL

<Tip>
Use a small paper size until the position, order, and history tabs behave exactly the way you expect.
</Tip>
