# Order and Position Management

TradeNet exposes current execution state in the Trade pane and the bottom order-management dock.

## Bottom dock

The dock can be expanded, collapsed, and resized.

Its tabs are:

- **Positions**
- **Open Orders**
- **Order History**
- **Trade History**
- **Position History**
- **Strategies**

Current-position and open-order counts appear on their tabs.

## Positions

The Positions view shows active positions and supports position actions such as:

- Close the full position
- Partially close
- Edit take profit and stop loss
- Close all positions

Always verify the symbol and quantity before using a bulk action.

## Open Orders

The Open Orders view shows resting manual and child orders. Cancel individual orders or use supported bulk cancellation controls.

## History

Order, trade, and position history are separate because they answer different questions:

- **Order History** shows submitted order lifecycle
- **Trade History** shows completed fills
- **Position History** shows completed position records

History can be filtered to the focused pane's symbol or left on all symbols.

## Strategies

The Strategies tab monitors:

- Scaled orders
- TWAP
- Chase
- Iceberg
- Swarm
- Armed Lua algos

Advanced strategies show progress, child-order counts, next action, exit policy, status, details, child ledger, and recent activity.

## Pause, cancel, and panic

Supported strategy controls include:

- Pause
- Resume
- Cancel one strategy
- Cancel all strategies
- Panic by symbol
- Panic all

Panic controls are intentionally armed before firing.

## Kill switch

Disable trading from the Trade panel when you need to stop new execution actions. The top bar displays **HALTED** while the switch is active.

The kill switch is broader than canceling one order. It blocks new execution through the shared trading route.
