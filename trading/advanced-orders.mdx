# Advanced Orders

Advanced orders break one intention into managed child orders.

During the public beta, normal accounts run them through paper trading.

## Enable strategy orders

Open the **Strategies** tab in the bottom dock and enable strategy orders, or select an advanced ticket from the Trade pane.

## Scaled

Scaled creates a ladder of limit orders across a price range.

Configure:

- Total target size
- Low and high price
- Number of rungs
- Price distribution
- Size distribution
- Reduce-only
- Exit policy

Use it when you want to build or reduce exposure across a zone instead of at one price.

## TWAP

TWAP divides the target into scheduled slices over time.

Configure:

- Duration
- Number of slices
- Market or Chase child type
- Optional price guard
- Timeout and failure behavior
- Reduce-only
- Exit policy

## Chase

Chase manages a limit order relative to the market.

Configure:

- Own-side or aggressive opposite-side peg
- Maintain or trail mode
- Tick or percentage distance
- Reprice timing
- Give-up behavior
- Reduce-only
- Exit policy

## Iceberg

Iceberg exposes only a smaller visible child while working a larger target.

Configure:

- Total target size
- Visible child size
- Fixed or pegged price mode
- Fixed price or offset
- Reduce-only
- Exit policy

## Swarm

Swarm splits the target into many smaller child orders.

Configure:

- Number of lots
- Irregular sizing
- Reduce-only
- Exit policy

## Exit policy

Advanced orders are managed by the client.

Choose what should happen when the app or tab closes:

- **Close on exit** cancels the managed strategy's remaining work
- **Keep open** leaves already placed venue orders open where the active route supports it

For paper beta use, keep the desktop app open while a strategy is active.

## Monitoring

The Strategies dock shows:

- Target, placed, and filled amount
- Progress
- Child orders
- Status
- Next action
- Exit policy
- Activity and errors

You can pause, resume, cancel, or panic from the same tab.
