# Paper Trading

The public beta is paper-first.

Normal beta accounts use TradeNet's local simulated execution engine for:

- Manual market and limit orders
- Position management
- Take-profit and stop-loss orders
- Advanced orders
- Lua algo intents

The interface is designed to exercise the same workflows without sending real exchange orders.

## Open the Trade panel

Click **Trade** in the left rail or add a Trade pane.

Confirm that the panel identifies the route as simulated or paper before placing an order.

## Choose the market

The Trade pane follows its selected symbol. If it is linked to the chart, changing the shared market updates both.

## Set leverage and margin mode

Choose:

- Leverage
- Isolated or cross margin mode

In simulation, these settings affect the paper position and liquidation calculations. They do not change a real exchange account.

## Enter size

TradeNet sizes the manual ticket with **margin USD**.

Approximate position notional is:

```text
margin USD x leverage = position notional
```

The order preview shows the resulting notional and quantity before confirmation.

## Place a first paper order

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose Market or Limit">
    Use Market for immediate simulated execution or Limit for a resting order.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Buy or Sell">
    Buy opens or increases long exposure. Sell opens or increases short exposure unless reduce-only is enabled.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set size and risk">
    Enter margin USD, leverage, margin mode, and optional TP or SL levels.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the preview">
    Check symbol, side, quantity, notional, order type, and risk settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Submit the paper order, then monitor it in the Trade pane or bottom dock.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Global kill switch

The Trade panel includes a global trading kill switch. When trading is disabled, the top bar shows **HALTED**.

The kill switch applies to manual orders, advanced orders, and Lua-driven order intents.

## Live execution

Live Bitunix routing exists for specifically approved accounts. It is restricted during the public beta and is not part of this quick-start flow.

<Warning>
Paper fills are a simulation. They do not reproduce exchange queue priority, full depth, slippage, latency, partial fills, or every liquidation behavior.
</Warning>
